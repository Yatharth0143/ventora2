## What the brief shows

The one-page validation brief confirms a large founder pool and real pressure on founders, but it does not give a rupee market size.

- **Founder pool:** DPIIT recognised 55,200+ startups in FY2025-26, and the total passed 2.23 lakh on 31 Mar 2026 ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=555314)).
- **Investor attention:** DocSend data puts the average time spent on a deck at 2 min 30 s (Jun 2024), with a record low of 2 min 18 s ([DocSend](https://www.docsend.com/index/)).
- **Funding pressure:** Tracxn counted 1,518 funding rounds in 2025, down about 39%, and seed funding fell 30% to $1.1B ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/27/india-startup-funding-hits-11b-in-2025-as-investors-grow-more-selective)).
- **Language:** 57% of Indian internet users prefer regional-language content ([Business Today, Kantar-IAMAI](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/indias-internet-revolution-key-insights-from-kantar-and-iamai-report-461043-2025-01-16)). This covers all users, not founders' decks.
- **Channel:** 213 incubators were approved under the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme as of Dec 2024 ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc202521494101.pdf)).
- **Competitors:** 1752vc, Ada Ventures Deck Genius, DocSend, Visible, Signal, LetsVenture and YC co-founder matching, taken from public page excerpts and not tested hands-on.

## What the brief does not have

The brief has no rupee market size, no proof that the gap is open, and no proof of demand.

- **No rupee market size.** The earlier figure of 811 incubators and accelerators had no source, so it was removed.
- **No proof the gap is open.** Whether competitors lack Hindi support or quote-level checks has not been tested hands-on, so "open market" is still an assumption.
- **No proof of demand.** Willingness to pay can only come from the founder and incubator conversations.

## A sizing you can defend today

The two ceilings below total about Rs 5.3 crore a year. The counts are verified; the prices and the 5% are assumptions to label as such.

| Segment | Formula | Per year (Rs) | Input status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founders, ceiling** | 55,200 new startups x Rs 499 per audit | about 2.75 crore | Count verified; price is an ASSUMPTION |
| **Incubators, ceiling** | 213 SISFS incubators x Rs 1.2 lakh per year | about 2.56 crore | Count verified; price is an ASSUMPTION |
| **Founders, year-1 example** | 5% of 55,200 = 2,760 audits x Rs 499 | about 13.8 lakh | The 5% is an ASSUMPTION |

> Both ceilings assume every startup or incubator buys, so a realistic first year is a small share of them. Replace Rs 499 and Rs 1.2 lakh with your final prices once you have tested them.

## What this means as a market specialist

On its own, contradiction checks on first-time founders' decks are a small niche, and an investor will do this arithmetic in seconds. Do not hide it; turn it into the scale story.

1. **Wedge:** contradiction checks on first-time founders' decks.
2. **Expansion:** other documents (financial projections, grant proposals, loan applications) and other Indian languages, which the architecture already allows.
3. **Proof later:** each expansion needs its own sizing and evidence, so mark it "roadmap" until you have it.

## What would prove the market is open

Only your own evidence can prove it; desk research cannot.

- [ ] Three founder calls, each written up as: what we assumed, what we found, what we changed.
- [ ] A hands-on test of 1752vc and Ada Deck Genius with a Hindi deck, with screenshots.
- [ ] One incubator conversation asking whether they would pay Rs 1.2 lakh a year.
